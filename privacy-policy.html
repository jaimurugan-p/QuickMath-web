Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Last Updated: November 13, 2026
Developer: JaiDroid Labs
Contact Email: labs@jaidroid.com

QuickMath (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how QuickMath handles personal information, device data, and media files.

Current app version: QuickMath has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Advertising is planned for a future version. Before ads or other third-party data processing are introduced, this policy and the app's disclosures will be updated.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Photos, Videos, and Files (Media)

  • Purpose: QuickMath accesses device photos and videos so you can select media to import, hide, and encrypt in private vaults.
  • On-device encryption: Imported media is encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM. PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derives the encryption key from your PIN using a unique random salt.
  • Media access: QuickMath does not scan or browse your media for unrelated purposes, and does not transmit media to developer-owned servers.

B. Passcodes and Security Credentials

Vault PINs and recovery-question answers are not stored in plaintext. They are salted with cryptographically random 128-bit salts and hashed on-device using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. They are not transmitted from your device. Encryption keys are held in memory while a vault is unlocked and cleared when it is locked, subject to device memory-management limitations.

C. Google Account Information (Optional Cloud Sync)

If you connect Google Drive for backup, QuickMath authenticates with your Google Account using Google Play Services OAuth 2.0. QuickMath receives your Google email address and display name and shows them in the app to identify the connected backup account. This account information is used for the backup feature and is not sent to developer-owned servers or used for advertising.

2. Google Drive API and Limited Use Disclosure

QuickMath optionally backs up and restores encrypted vault items using Google Drive.

  • Restricted AppData scope: QuickMath requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata for Drive access. This scope permits access to QuickMath's hidden Application Data folder, not your personal files, documents, photos, or spreadsheets in the regular Drive area.
  • Limited use: QuickMath's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Google account information and credentials are used only for backup and restore. They are not sold or used for advertising. Vault backup files sent to Drive are encrypted on your device before upload; encryption keys are not uploaded.

Google operates the Drive authorization and storage services and processes information under Google's privacy policy and terms. The Drive AppData folder is hidden from the regular Drive file list. See Google's AppData documentation.

3. Third-Party Sharing and Analytics

  • No advertising in the current version: QuickMath contains no third-party advertising SDKs. Ads are planned for a future version; this policy and the app's disclosures will be revised before ads are introduced.
  • No analytics or crash reporting: The current version does not include behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling of data: We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal data or vault content. If you enable Drive backup, encrypted backup files are sent to Google as the storage provider for that feature, as described above. We do not share Google account information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

4. Data Retention, Shredding, and Deletion

Local vault data remains on your device until you delete it or clear QuickMath's app storage. Optional Drive backups remain in the hidden AppData folder until deleted by QuickMath's synced cleanup or removed through Google's app-data controls. If a device is offline, cloud cleanup occurs when the app can sync again.

  1. Delete individual media: Unlock QuickMath by entering your PIN and long-pressing =. Long-press a photo or video to enter multi-select mode, select items, tap the trash/delete icon, and confirm. When Drive sync is connected, QuickMath runs a cleanup pass to delete matching encrypted cloud files.
  2. Delete an entire vault: Unlock the vault, tap the Settings gear, scroll to Delete This Vault, and confirm with Shred & Delete. This removes that vault's encrypted files, thumbnails, albums, and database records.
  3. Clear all local app data: On your device, open Settings → Apps → QuickMath → Storage & cache → Clear storage (labels vary by device). This removes local vaults, PINs, and databases, but does not by itself delete Drive backups.
  4. Delete Google Drive cloud data: In a browser, open drive.google.com, then go to Settings → Manage apps. Find QuickMath, open Options, and select Delete hidden app data. Confirm the deletion. Google may change menu labels. Deleted backups cannot be restored.

QuickMath's local shred routine uses zero-fill overwriting before removal. Flash storage controllers and wear-leveling may prevent software from guaranteeing that every physical storage cell has been overwritten.

Keep your PIN and required recovery credentials safe. If you lose credentials needed to decrypt your vault, your encrypted data may not be recoverable.

5. Permissions Used by QuickMath

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+): select and import photos and videos into the vault.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 14+): supports partial photo and video access when you select specific media.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and earlier): reads device media on older Android versions.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and earlier): exports restored files on older devices.
  • INTERNET: communicates with Google Drive for backup and restore when you enable or use cloud sync.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: checks Wi-Fi/mobile connectivity so paused sync can resume when the device reconnects.
  • VIBRATE: provides tactile feedback when tapping calculator keys or long-pressing =.

QuickMath does not request camera, microphone/audio recording, location, contacts, phone, SMS, or biometric permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

QuickMath is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
